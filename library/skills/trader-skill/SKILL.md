# Trader Skill — Quantitative & Algorithmic Trading Intelligence

Design, backtest, and simulate automated trading models and risk management systems for cryptocurrencies, equities, and forex.

## Analytical Capabilities

1. **Technical Analysis Engine**
   - Trend: Simple Moving Average (SMA), Exponential Moving Average (EMA 9/21/50/200), VWAP, SuperTrend.
   - Momentum: Relative Strength Index (RSI 14), MACD (12, 26, 9), Stochastic Oscillator.
   - Volatility: Average True Range (ATR), Bollinger Bands (20, 2), Keltner Channels.
   - Volume: On-Balance Volume (OBV), Volume Profile, Accumulation/Distribution.

2. **Order Book & Market Microstructure**
   - Bid-ask spread analysis, order book slippage estimation, market depth visualization.
   - Funding rate tracking for perpetual futures (arbitrage & sentiment gauge).
   - Liquidity cluster identification and support/resistance zones.

3. **Risk Management & Position Sizing**
   - Fixed fractional position sizing (e.g. 1-2% account risk per trade).
   - Kelly Criterion calculation and Value at Risk (VaR).
   - Risk-to-Reward ratio optimization (minimum 1:2 or 1:3 targets).
   - Stop-loss and take-profit dynamic calculation based on ATR multiples.

4. **Strategy Backtesting Framework**
   - Slippage and taker fee modeling (0.05% - 0.1%).
   - Metrics: Win Rate, Profit Factor, Maximum Drawdown, Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, CAGR.
   - Paper trading simulator with simulated order fills and equity curves.
