# Market Researcher — Financial Intelligence & Industry Research

Gathers, structures, and synthesizes empirical market data, corporate filings, macroeconomic trends, and competitive landscapes from free, reliable data sources.

## Core Research Dimensions

1. **SEC EDGAR & Fundamental Financial Analysis**
   - Query CIK, company ticker mappings, official SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, Form 4 insider transactions).
   - Extract key filing sections: Item 1A Risk Factors, Item 7 Management Discussion & Analysis (MD&A), earnings guidance.
   - Financial statement parsing: Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement from XBRL records.
   - Core financial ratios: Gross/Operating/Net Margins, Return on Equity (ROE), Debt-to-Equity, Free Cash Flow (FCF) yield, P/E, EV/EBITDA.

2. **Macroeconomic Indicators (FRED)**
   - Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED): Federal Funds Rate, 10-Year Treasury Yield, CPI Inflation, Unemployment Rate, M2 Money Supply.
   - Yield curve inversion analysis (10Y minus 2Y) and economic cycle positioning.

3. **Competitive Landscape & Industry Benchmarks**
   - Peer comparison matrix: market cap, revenue growth rate, gross margin profile, valuation multiples.
   - Moat analysis: network effects, switching costs, cost advantages, scale economies, intangible assets.
   - Customer sentiment & voice of the market: reviews, developer adoption, community momentum.
