# LinkedIn Agent — Organic Content & Professional Authority

Eleven specialized skills to run a LinkedIn account with high signal-to-noise ratio, zero AI slop, and battle-tested engagement formulas.

## The Eleven Commands & Modes

1. **/li-post**: Transform one core insight into a high-performing post. Generates 3 hook options from 21 verified formulas (Contrarian, The Transformation, The Mistake, The Teardown, The Math, etc.) + 1 full humanized draft.
2. **/li-human**: Strip AI fingerprint from text:
   - Removes 113 known AI slop terms (delve, tapestry, testament, vital, multifaceted, elevate, seamless, beacon, game-changer).
   - Eliminates invisible unicode watermark characters (zero-width spaces, word joiners, soft hyphens).
   - Replaces artificial punctuation (em dashes replaced with commas/periods; curly quotes normalized).
   - Evaluates on 5-point detection panel: Burstiness, Specificity, Slop Density, Fingerprint, Voice.
3. **/li-profile**: Audit a LinkedIn profile against the 100-point rubric across 12 distinct components (Headline, About/Hook, Proof, Experience, Banner, Featured items) and output line-by-line rewrites.
4. **/li-comment**: Craft substantive, insightful comments on target industry posts across 9 engagement categories (Add nuance, Real-world data, Counter-example, Framework extension, Tactical question). Never "Great post!".
5. **/li-reply**: Triage and respond to comments under your posts, sorting into Leads, Substance, Peers, Support, and Noise.
6. **/li-plan**: Construct a weekly posting calendar (what to post, ideal timestamps, and the 10 target accounts to engage with first).
7. **/li-carousel**: Multi-slide PDF document posts with compelling cover slides, 5-7 punchy content frames, and high-converting CTA.
8. **/li-repurpose**: Convert long-form content (YouTube video, podcast, blog post, code PR) into 5 distinct standalone posts.
9. **/li-dm**: 200-character personalized connection invite note, initial message, and 2 value-add follow-up steps.
10. **/li-inbox**: Triage incoming direct messages into Lead, Recruiter, Peer, Ask, or Automated Spam.
11. **/li-audit**: Post-mortem analytics analysis on past posts to measure engagement rate and reach multiplier instead of vanity impressions.
