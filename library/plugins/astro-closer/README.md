# Closer Pack

11 skills that run a small sales team: find and rank leads, cold openers, follow-ups, a research brief before each call, booking calls, live call coaching from captions, proposals, contracts, win-backs, a deal pipeline and a monthly forecast.

> **Read first.** The tools never send anything. Each email, proposal or agreement goes out only after your own yes for that one message (as a Gmail draft you send, or through a Gmail MCP server you added), and the log steps refuse anything else. Two helpers go online with no login or keys: prospector/leads reads one public page per business website, slowly and identifying itself, and researcher/brief reads up to 4 public pages of one business. Cold email has to follow the law where you send it: in the US (CAN-SPAM) every email needs your postal address and a plain opt-out, which the opener's footer adds; in the UK, EU and Canada cold email to people needs a lawful basis or consent first. Anyone who says no goes on the suppression list and is never emailed again.

Adapted from [closer-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/closer-skill) by Jake Schincariol (MIT). See NOTICE.md.

## Use

Ask the agent for the job in plain words ("write chapters for this transcript"), or name the skill.
Some skills run helper tools that are built into A.S.T.R.O AI (1.9.1 or later); nothing extra to install.
Some helpers read public web data (they say which site in their help text).
