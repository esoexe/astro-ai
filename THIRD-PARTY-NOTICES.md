**Library packs:** each pack in `library/plugins/` that is adapted from someone else's work ships the upstream licence and a list of changes in its own `NOTICE.md` (for 1.9.5: the Design Pack with Anthropic's frontend-design under Apache-2.0 in `skills/frontend-design/LICENSE.txt`, the 3D Pack from CloudAI-X/threejs-skills and the Motion Pack from greensock/gsap-skills, both MIT; for 1.9.6: the Motion Pack's StringTune skill, written in our own words from Fiddle.Digital's public StringTune docs and its MIT-licensed npm package). The community catalog also reads Docker's docker/mcp-registry repo (MIT) to list its servers; it copies only names, descriptions and image names.

**Downloaded on request, not bundled:** the optional *Natural voice* installs Piper (MIT, https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) and voice
models from `rhasspy/piper-voices` with their own licenses, shown before you install: en_US ljspeech (public domain),
fr_FR siwis (CC BY 4.0), ar_JO kareem (personal use only, as published by its author). Each download is checked against a pinned SHA-256.

`packtools/pyre.h` (the pack tools' regular expressions, string predicates and `html_unescape`) is A.S.T.R.O AI's own C++ code that
follows the documented behaviour of Python's `re` and `html` modules. No CPython code is included; some inputs in
`tools/unicode/re_test.cpp` follow cases from CPython's `Lib/test/test_re.py` (PSF License 2.0, https://docs.python.org/3/license.html).

**Installed by you, not bundled:** the Astro Packs video tools (`master-edit/probe`, `deadspace`, `captions`, `stopmotion`, A.S.T.R.O AI's own
C++ code) run FFmpeg/ffprobe (LGPL-2.1+ or GPL, depending on the build; https://ffmpeg.org) and, only for transcription, whisper.cpp
(MIT, https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) with a Whisper model in ggml format (MIT, OpenAI). They are never downloaded automatically.

AI providers listed in `providers.json` are third-party services with their own terms. A.S.T.R.O AI is not affiliated with them.

**Astro RE (Reverse page, `re` agent tool, `astro re`)** is A.S.T.R.O AI's own C++ code in `astro_re.inc`. Its idea and its
"findings with evidence and unknowns" approach are inspired by REA by morluto (https://github.com/morluto/rea, MIT). No REA code
is included. The optional **Add REA (MCP)** button only writes `npx -y rea-agents@6.8.0 mcp` into your MCP settings; npm downloads REA
under its own MIT license when it first runs. The analyzers offered in Settings > Tools are downloaded on request from their official
releases, with their own licenses: rizin (LGPL-3.0, https://github.com/rizinorg/rizin), JADX (Apache-2.0, https://github.com/skylot/jadx)
and Ghidra (Apache-2.0, https://github.com/NationalSecurityAgency/ghidra).
