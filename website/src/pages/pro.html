A.S.T.R.O AI Pro

Autonomous project watch sentinel, real-time dual-presence bridge between PC and Android, and proactive voice calling contact skill. Engineered for zero-delay operations.

Architecture and Core Pillars

A.S.T.R.O AI Pro elevates standard reactive assistance into an autonomous, proactive engineering orchestrator operating concurrently across desktop workstation and mobile device.

1. Proactive Contact Skill

When autonomous tasks encounter breaking build errors, test regressions, or deployment authorization gates, the agent proactively calls or messages your phone via Bland AI voice dispatch, telephony, or high-priority notifications.

2. Instant Dual-Presence Bridge

When your PC is online, both PC and phone operate concurrently on the primary server hub. If your PC is shut down, Android automatically switches to its internal local engine without dropping active tasks. When the PC returns online, tasks synchronize back automatically.

3. Autonomous Project Sentinel

Continuous background watcher monitoring source integrity, unit test suites, subagent progress, and task stalls. Features self-healing loops that attempt local fixes before escalating to the user.

4. Executive HUD & Multi-Agent Swarm

A distinct tactical interface with live orbital radar telemetry, multi-node ping monitors, and specialized engines: System Architect, Code Auditor, Autonomous Sentinel, and Contact Bridge.

Dual-Presence Connectivity Rules

  • PC Online (Primary Hub): Android connects instantly over local network or Tailscale. Sessions, agent jobs, and files are shared across both devices concurrently.
  • PC Offline (Instant Auto-Failover): Android detects dropped connection within two heartbeat intervals and activates its internal C++ engine. Active queues transfer to local execution with zero dropped tasks.
  • PC Restoration: Continuous background reconnector discovers the PC server upon reboot, handshakes with pairing keys, and syncs workspace checkpoints back to the workstation.
