An optional plan, maybe.

The free app stays free and open source. We are exploring an optional paid plan to fund the project. Nothing is for sale today, and we won't charge anyone until it's real.

What stays free, always

  • The whole app: One Brain, the Workspace, the Agent, voice, extensions, desktop, terminal and Android preview.
  • All free models, your own keys and local models.
  • The source code, under MIT, Apache-2.0 or GPL-3.0 (your choice).

Ideas for Pro (tell us which matter)

  • Hosted models with higher limits, so answers don't pause when free tiers run out.
  • Faster answers from stronger paid models in the team.
  • Sync across devices without running your own server.
  • Priority support and help setting it up for a team or class.

Prices aren't set. If it happens, it will be a simple monthly plan with a free trial and cancel-anytime, sold through a payment provider that works in your country.
