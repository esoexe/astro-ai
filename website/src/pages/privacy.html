Privacy

Privacy, plainly.

Short version: your data stays on your computer, there's no account and no tracking, and your questions go only to the AI providers that answer them.

The app

What stays on your computer

Your conversations, notes, tasks, projects, knowledge files, settings and API keys are stored in a folder on your own computer (see where). We don't run servers that store them, and we can't see them.

What leaves your computer

  • Your questions and their context (relevant chat history, attached files, knowledge files and, in the Workspace or Agent, the parts of your project being worked on) are sent to the AI providers that generate the answer. With One Brain, that's every provider on the team. With a single model, it's only that model's provider.
  • Web mode and the Reader fetch the pages you ask for, or search results, from the internet.
  • The Agent can access the internet and run commands as part of a job you give it.

Each AI provider handles your data under its own terms and privacy policy, and free tiers sometimes use prompts to improve their models. Don't send passwords, personal details about others, or confidential material you wouldn't share with those providers. Settings shows each provider's privacy notes where we know them.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in or email address needed for the app (the optional A.S.T.R.O API account is separate)
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising, and no automatic reporting unless you turn it on (see below)
  • No selling or sharing of data

Error reports and feedback (only if you choose)

Everything here is off by default and goes to one insert-only table at our database provider (Supabase). The public key can only add rows, never read them, and we don't store IP addresses.

  • Anonymous error reports (Settings › Data & privacy, or the welcome screen). When on, app errors and engine crashes are sent: the error message, where in A.S.T.R.O AI's code it happened, the screen you were on, your app version, system type (for example windows-x64), interface language and browser version. Keys, tokens, e-mail addresses and the user name in file paths are removed first. Your chats, prompts, files and projects are never included. Settings shows exactly what a report contains before you turn it on. Turn it off at any time; administrators can block it entirely with ASTRO_REPORTS=off.
  • Send feedback sends only what you type, plus your app version and system type, and an e-mail address if you add one so we can answer.
  • "This answer didn't work" is saved on your computer; the question and answer are shared with us only if you tick "share" in that form.
  • Beta tester and Pro waitlist forms on this website send the e-mail address and the answers you enter.

We use these reports only to fix bugs and plan features. Reports are deleted after 12 months. To have your feedback or signup deleted earlier, send a deletion request with the same e-mail address through Send feedback in the app or the beta form.

Folders, extensions and coding tools

  • Folders you open are read and changed in place on your computer. In Ask mode (the default for folders) the agent asks before it changes files or runs commands. The parts of your project the agent works on are sent to the AI providers answering it, as described above.
  • Browsing extensions sends your search terms to the public registries: the MCP registry (registry.modelcontextprotocol.io) and Open VSX (open-vsx.org). Installing a plugin downloads it from GitHub. Those services see your IP address, like any website you visit.
  • MCP servers, plugins and hooks you install run on your computer, or connect to the remote address you added. They may contact their own services, under their own terms. Servers and hooks from a project only start after you trust them, and you can stop or untrust them at any time.
  • Coding CLIs such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or DeepSeek Harness only run if you have installed them, and then follow their own privacy policies.

Local engine

A.S.T.R.O AI runs a small engine on your computer that only listens on 127.0.0.1, so other devices on your network can't connect to it.

Android app

On Android the same engine runs inside the app, on your phone. Your chats, files and settings stay in the app's private storage and are excluded from Android backups. Each time the app starts it creates a new random key that only the app's own window receives, so other apps on your phone cannot use the engine even though it listens on 127.0.0.1. The app asks for two permissions only: internet (to reach the AI models) and the microphone (asked the first time you use voice input, never used in the background). Voice uses your phone's own speech services; depending on your phone, speech recognition may be processed by Google or your phone maker. Files you save go to Downloads/A.S.T.R.O AI.

Microphone

Voice features use your operating system's speech services and only listen while voice is active. Depending on your system, speech recognition may be processed by your OS vendor's services.

This website

This site is plain static pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party scripts, and all fonts and images are served from this site. The only exceptions are the account page, which talks to our sign-in service (see below), and the beta and Pro waitlist forms, which send what you enter to our database (see above). The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operations.

Accounts and the A.S.T.R.O API

Accounts are optional and only needed for the A.S.T.R.O API. They are run on Supabase (our database and sign-in provider). When you create one, we store:

  • Your email address, plus your GitHub username if you continue with GitHub. Used only to sign you in.
  • Your Astro keys, as one-way SHA-256 hashes, with a name and the date each was created and last used.
  • Provider keys you add, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, plus their last 4 characters so you can tell them apart. They are decrypted only to forward your own requests to that provider.
  • A daily request count, used for the limit and your usage chart.

We don't store your prompts or the answers. They pass straight through to the provider you choose, whose own privacy policy then applies. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie. You can delete your account and all of this data at any time from the account page.

Children

A.S.T.R.O AI isn't directed at children under 13. AI providers set their own minimum ages, and you should follow them.
