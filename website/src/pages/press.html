At a glance

Boilerplate

Astro AI is a free AI coding platform and desktop app for Windows, Linux and macOS, where a team of free AI models works as one. Developers open any folder, choose Plan, Ask or Auto permissions, and let the agent plan, write, test and review code. It reads the rules, skills, MCP servers and plugins already used with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and OpenCode. Its One Brain chat sends each question to every available model and merges their reviewed drafts into one answer. No account, analytics or telemetry.
