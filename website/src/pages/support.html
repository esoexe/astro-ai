Support

Keep it free.

A.S.T.R.O AI is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking. Donations pay for test devices, code signing and time. Everything stays free either way.

Donate

{{support:links}}

Donations are optional and don't unlock anything. They help pay for test hardware (Windows on ARM, Android phones, Macs), hosting and the time to keep up with free model changes.

Free ways to help (worth just as much)

  • Become a beta tester. Try new versions on your own computer or phone and tell us what breaks. This is what we need most.
  • Turn on anonymous error reports in the app (Settings › Data & privacy). Only errors and crashes are sent, never chats. What exactly is sent.
  • Report bugs and ideas with Send feedback in the app, or on GitHub.
  • Star the repository on GitHub and tell a friend who codes.
  • Contribute: translations (French and Arabic exist, more are welcome), docs, code. See the repository's README.

What about a paid version?

The core app stays free and open source. We are considering an optional Pro plan for people who want more (for example hosted models with higher limits). It does not exist yet; join the waitlist to tell us what you would pay for.
