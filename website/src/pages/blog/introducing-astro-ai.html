Introducing Astro AI 1.0.

A free desktop app where a team of AI models answers as one, with a code workspace, an agent and voice built in.

Today we're releasing Astro AI 1.0 for Windows and Linux, plus a build kit for macOS. It's free, it doesn't need an account, and it works the first time you open it.

The idea

There are dozens of capable AI models with free tiers, spread across dozens of providers. Each one is good at different things, and each makes different mistakes. Most apps make you pick one. We wondered what would happen if you didn't have to.

So Astro AI's default is One Brain. Your question goes to every available model at once. They draft answers, review each other's work, and one model merges the best of it into a single reply. Under the reply, you can see how many models took part and how confident they were. In our testing, a question about how rockets reach orbit got an answer from 19 models working together.

What's in 1.0

  • Chat with One Brain or any single model, in General, Compare or Web mode, in the language you choose.
  • Workspace: a code editor where an AI engineering team writes complete files, runs them and fixes failures. Every change is saved as a version, so you can always undo.
  • Agent: hand over a whole job. It plans with the team, then runs commands, edits files and checks its work, with every step logged and a checkpoint to roll back to.
  • Live voice, Model Lab, Reader, Notes, Tasks, Knowledge files and a calculator.
  • A calm interface in light and dark, and a Windows installer we're a little too proud of.

Free, honestly

Astro AI costs nothing, and the 26 models it starts with come from free tiers that need no key. You can add free keys from more than 50 supported providers to grow the team. Keys are stored only on your computer.

"Free" has limits, though, and we'd rather be upfront about them. Free tiers have rate limits and daily caps that providers set and change. When a provider runs out, the rest of the team carries on, but on a busy day you may notice fewer minds at work.

What it isn't (yet)

  • It isn't instant. A full One Brain answer usually takes a few minutes, because it waits for the whole team. Here's why, and how to go faster when you need to.
  • It isn't infallible. A team catches more mistakes than one model, but it can still be wrong. Double-check anything important.
  • It isn't signed yet. Windows will show a SmartScreen warning the first time. Each download has a published SHA-256 checksum so you can check it.
  • There's no ready-made Mac app yet. The macOS kit builds a universal app on your Mac in about a minute.

Private by default

There's no account, no analytics and no telemetry. Your chats, notes and keys stay in a folder on your computer. Questions go only to the AI providers that answer them. The privacy page covers the details in plain language.
