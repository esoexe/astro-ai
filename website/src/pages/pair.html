Make a pairing code on your computer

  1. On your computer, open A.S.T.R.O AI (desktop app 1.8.1 or later) and go to Settings → Phone.
  2. Turn on Let my phone connect and click Pair a phone.
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera, or type the address and code in the app under Connect to my computer.

Don't have the app?

A.S.T.R.O AI for Android 1.8.2 or later, for Android 8.0+ on 64-bit ARM phones: download the APK. Not on Google Play.

Your phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi or on a private network such as Tailscale. Phone access is off until you turn it on, and you can remove a paired phone in Settings → Phone at any time.
