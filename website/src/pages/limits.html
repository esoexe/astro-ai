Honest limits

What it can't do (yet).

A.S.T.R.O AI is free because it runs on free tiers of AI platforms. That comes with real limits. Here they are, without marketing.

Free is not unlimited

  • Every free platform has daily or per-minute limits. When one is used up, its models rest and A.S.T.R.O AI uses the others. On a busy day, fewer minds join an answer, and in the worst case you'll see "limit reached" until the daily reset.
  • Without any key you get {{limits:count}}. Adding free keys (about a minute each, in Settings › Providers) adds more minds and fewer pauses.
  • Free tiers change without notice. Models get removed, limits get tighter, and a platform can close its free tier. We update the list in each release, but some models may disappear between releases.
  • Local models are the fallback. If you install Ollama, A.S.T.R.O AI uses it automatically when every cloud model is busy, or for everything with Local only (Settings › Brain). It's free and private, but uses your computer's memory and is slower on most laptops.

Speed

  • One Brain is slow on purpose. Every model works on your question and finishes before the merged answer is written, so a typical answer takes 15 to 30 seconds (our benchmark median is about 21 s). You see a quick first draft while you wait, a count of minds done and a time estimate. "Answer now" stops waiting for the slow minds.
  • Picking a single model in the model menu is much faster, but you lose the cross-checking.

Accuracy

  • Answers can be wrong. Merging many models catches many mistakes, not all. Check anything important: medical, legal, financial, safety.
  • The Agent can make bad changes. It saves a checkpoint before every job (one click to undo), checks its own claims before it says "done", and a second model reviews its work. It is still software written by AI: review the changes before you ship them.
  • A.S.T.R.O AI is not AGI and does not learn from your chats.

Privacy

  • Your chats and files stay on your computer, but your questions go to the AI platforms that answer them, and some free tiers may log prompts or use them for training. The tables below show what each platform says. Don't send secrets or other people's personal data. Use Local only for private work.
  • Details: Privacy.

Safety of the Agent

  • In Auto mode the Agent runs commands on your computer with your permissions. Risky commands (deleting outside the project, formatting disks, piping downloads into a shell) are blocked or need your approval, and Isolated runs work in a copy of the project. It is not a full operating-system sandbox. For untrusted code, use Ask mode, an isolated run, or a virtual machine or container.

Not finished yet

  • Unsigned installers. Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper warn on first launch. We have applied for free open-source code signing; until it's approved, check the SHA-256 checksums on the download page.
  • Android is new in 1.8.0: install the APK from the download page (not on Google Play yet). It was tested on a computer, not yet on many real phones. A stock phone has no bash, Node or compilers, so Workspace commands that need them say "not found", and the Agent sandbox is not available. 64-bit ARM phones only (Android 8.0+). There is no iOS app.
  • ARM64 desktop builds (Windows on ARM, Linux arm64) are built automatically but have not been tested on real ARM devices yet. Windows ARM64 needs Windows 11 on ARM.
  • Voice uses your system's speech voices unless you install the offline Natural voice (Settings › Voice; English, French and Arabic, desktop only, no Windows ARM build). Speech recognition always uses your system or browser, so quality depends on your device.
  • Small team, few real-device testers. Bugs on hardware we don't have are likely. Become a beta tester to help.
